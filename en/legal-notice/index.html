Method

“Based on ISO” means that Syntropica's calculation method is built on the principles and requirements of ISO 14064-2. The calculation method was applied in a pilot project that obtained a positive validation opinion from Bureau Veritas Italia S.p.A. in 2026. The opinion is issued to the project's client, a forest operator to which the founders provided methodological consulting; it concerns that client's project (description, baseline, monitoring and reporting procedures) against ISO 14064-2:2019, in accordance with ISO 14064-3, and confirms the reasonableness of the assumptions adopted. It is not a certification of Syntropica or of the method; actual results may differ from forecasts. Later ones will be issued, case by case, to the clients of new projects. Syntropica holds the method by assignment and licence from the founders. Validation of the reporting method is planned. Verification of the quantities of each project is carried out case by case by the body chosen by the client.

The figures reported are a representative average summary of interventions and are intended for general information; although relevant to the situation, they cannot on their own represent the complexity of a forest intervention.

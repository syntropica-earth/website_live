Privacy notice

pursuant to Art. 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) · Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Data controller

Syntropica S.r.l. SB, via Valgerola 7b, 23017 Morbegno (SO), Italy — VAT no. 01109100147 — PEC syntropica@pec.it — email contact@syntropica.earth.

2. Data processed

The data you enter in the contact form: full name, email, organisation, category ("I am…") and message. Technical browsing data strictly necessary for the operation and security of the website, logged by the hosting service GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

3. Purposes and legal basis

Replying to your enquiries: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in following up contact requests). Ensuring the security of the website: Art. 6(1)(f).

4. Provision of data

Providing your data is optional; without the data required by the form we cannot reply to you.

5. Processing methods and recipients

Data is processed electronically by authorised personnel. Form messages are received through Formspree, Inc.; the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Both act as data processors. Data is not disseminated, nor used for marketing or profiling purposes.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Formspree and GitHub are based in the United States. Transfers take place on the basis of an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR) or of appropriate safeguards (Art. 46 GDPR).

7. Retention

For as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and in any case no longer than 24 months from the last contact, unless a contractual relationship arises from it.

8. Your rights

You may at any time request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing and data portability, and object to processing (Arts. 15-22 GDPR), by writing to contact@syntropica.earth or to the PEC address.

9. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

10. Cookies

The website does not use cookies. It does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools; fonts are hosted on the website itself, with no requests to external services.

11. Changes

This notice may be updated; the date of last update is shown at the top.
